Opportunity

Test one narrow support flow.

  • Overflow or after-hours intake for one approved service category.
  • Collect service type, location, urgency and callback details consistently.
  • Produce a structured summary for the dispatcher instead of making technical decisions.
  • Escalate emergencies and exceptions according to Carini-approved rules.
Guardrails

Support, not replacement.

  • No autonomous technical diagnosis.
  • No promise of appointment availability without approval.
  • No change to Carini's existing customer communication policy.
  • No scale-up until a human-reviewed pilot meets agreed acceptance criteria.
Focused pilot

A measurable handoff from caller to dispatcher.

The pilot is designed to validate one workflow before broader automation. Baseline, routing rules and success criteria are agreed with Carini first.

1. Approved intake

Capture customer, service, location, urgency and callback information.

2. Rule-based routing

Apply Carini-approved priority and escalation rules without technical diagnosis.

3. Dispatcher summary

Deliver a consistent summary so the human team can confirm the next action.
